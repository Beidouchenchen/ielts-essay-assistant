# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 4

## Prompt

The diagram below shows the process for recycling plastic bottles.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### How plastic bottles are recycled

1. **Collection** — Plastic bottles are placed in recycling bins
2. **Transport** — A recycling truck collects the bottles
3. **Recycling centre: Sorting** — Bottles are sorted (correct ✓ vs incorrect ✗)
4. **Compressing into blocks** — Sorted bottles are compressed into large blocks
5. **Crushing** — Blocks are crushed into smaller pieces
6. **Producing plastic pellets** — Crushed plastic is processed into pellets
7. **Heating pellets to form raw material** — Pellets are heated to create raw material
8. **Raw material** — Raw material is stored on pallets
9. **Producing end products** — Raw material is used to make new products (bottles, clothing, containers, bags)

## Key Visual Features

- The process is a closed循环: bottles are collected, processed, and turned into new products
- Sorting step removes non-recyclable items
- Multiple stages of physical transformation: compressing → crushing → pellets → heating
- End products include a variety of items beyond just bottles
- The process involves both manual (sorting) and mechanical (crushing, heating) steps
