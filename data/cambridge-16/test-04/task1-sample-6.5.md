## Essay

This is a process by which plastic bottles are recycled.

Plastic bottles are one of the most utilised materials used in the world, and recycling is a hugely important topic for today's society. Let us look at how the recycling process is carried out.

Around the streets, in both houses and restaurants is possible to find yellow bins, to collect organic, and glass waste. The yellow bin is the one that we use for plastic, and after a couple of weeks a truck goes around cities to collect the waste inside these bins, which are taken to a recycling plant. In there, workers sort the waste by removing non-recyclable material, and once this has been completed, the recycling process begins.

Firstly, once the plastic has been sorted, it is then compressed into large blocks, and following that, these blocks are crushed, to facilitate the recycling and obtain a powder, which will be transformed into pellets, and following that, this material will be turned into raw material.

Going back to how this can help in the production of plastic bottles, it can finally be turned into a raw material.

Following this, in order to produce new plastic bottles, factories can use recycled pellets, which become bags, containers, T-shirts, toys, caps, and even more. As long as people keep sorting waste, the world will be a cleaner place.

If society keeps doing these tiny things, life in the whole planet will be much better. We are not doing enough to stop pollution, and separating the correct thing in the correct bin is an easy way to keep the environment safe.
