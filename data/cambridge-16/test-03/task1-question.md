# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 3

## Prompt

The plans below show the site of an airport now and how it will look after redevelopment next year.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

#### Current Layout (NOW)
- 8 gates (numbered 1-8)
- Walkway connecting gates to terminal
- Check-in area
- Café
- Security / Passport Control / Customs
- Departures and Arrivals sections

#### After Development
- 18 gates (numbered 1-18), expanded from 8
- New terminal building with expanded facilities
- Check-in area
- Bag drop (new)
- Café
- ATM (new)
- Sky train (new)
- Car hire (new)
- Shops added between departures and passport control

## Key Visual Features

- Gate capacity more than doubled (8 → 18)
- New facilities added: bag drop, ATM, sky train, car hire

- Overall expansion of the airport infrastructure
