## Essay

Today high levels of sugar are contained in many sources of food, especially in manufactured food. And, of course, eating so much sugar is not good for our health. It can cause a simple case, for example, but also worse problems, like the increasing level of sugar in blood. Some people suggest that sugary products should be more expensive, so people would buy less of them.

According to me, I think that is not the best one as sugary products include some types of food that we eat everyday, such as bread or pasta. These foods, the first one, are really important for many people, and the second one is also important for Italian people, for example. So, if the price of these foods were higher, some people wouldn't be able to buy the most important food for them. Just think that to poor people, who can only afford a few loaves of bread per day, what would they eat if we increased bread price?

I think that the best solution for this problem would be informing people about what they eat because sometimes we don't even know it. They have already done something similar to this, for example, about cigarettes, so I think we should do the same for food. It is possible to inform people about the ingredients of a particular food. Yet, many people spend some of their time reading labels, but probably they don't know the biggest part of the substances listed in it, as well not everybody knows that there is a specific order of the ingredients in the list. So something could be done to organise a system that "talks" to people not only about food, but also about the risks they could face if they ate too much of these substances. And we shouldn't forget about children and teenagers, who like to eat a lot, also in schools, because also children must be aware of what they eat. If teenagers, children can know what they have learned by those "conferences" at their parents, so the whole family would eat better.

To sum up, I think that it is not necessary to increase the prices of sugary food and that all we need is information, is to teach people to eat less sugary foods and, as a consequence, live better with less problems.
