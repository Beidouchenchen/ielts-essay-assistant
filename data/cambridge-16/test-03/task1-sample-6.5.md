## Essay

The given two maps illustrate the current layout of Southwest Airport and the planned changes after redevelopment next year.

Overall, the airport will undergo significant expansion, with the number of gates increasing from 8 to 18 and several new facilities being added. The terminal building will also be expanded to accommodate the increased capacity.

Currently, the airport has 8 gates connected to the terminal by a walkway. The terminal includes check-in, a café, security, passport control, and customs areas. Departures and arrivals are separated.

After development, the airport will have 18 gates, with the terminal building expanded considerably. New facilities will include a bag drop, an ATM, a sky train, and a car hire service. Shops will be added between the departures area and passport control. The walkway will be removed, and the gates will be directly connected to the expanded terminal. The security, passport control, and customs areas will remain in similar positions but will be part of the larger terminal structure.
