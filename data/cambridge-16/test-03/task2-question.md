# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 3

## Prompt

Many manufactured food and drink products contain high levels of sugar, which causes many health problems. Sugary products should be made more expensive to encourage people to consume less sugar.

Do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Opinion (agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** Taxing sugary products to reduce consumption
- **Position:** Agree or disagree that sugary products should be made more expensive
- **Key terms:** sugar, health problems, expensive, encourage, consume less
