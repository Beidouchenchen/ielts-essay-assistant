# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 1

## Prompt

The charts below show the changes in ownership of electrical appliances and amount of time spent doing housework in households in one country between 1920 and 2019.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Chart 1: Percentage of households with electrical appliances (1920–2019)

| Year | Washing machine | Refrigerator | Vacuum cleaner |
|------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1920 | 40% | 0% | 30% |
| 1940 | 50% | 10% | 40% |
| 1960 | 60% | 30% | 50% |
| 1980 | 70% | 60% | 70% |
| 2000 | 70% | 90% | 90% |
| 2019 | 75% | 100% | 100% |

### Chart 2: Number of hours of housework per week, per household (1920–2019)

| Year | Hours per week |
|------|---------------|
| 1920 | 50 |
| 1940 | 35 |
| 1960 | 18 |
| 1980 | 15 |
| 2000 | 12 |
| 2019 | 10 |

*housework = washing clothes, preparing meals, cleaning

## Key Visual Features

- Washing machine ownership rose steadily from 40% to 75% over the century
- Refrigerator ownership saw the most dramatic increase, from 0% in 1920 to 100% by 2019
- Vacuum cleaner ownership increased from 30% to 100%, with a sharp rise between 1960 and 1980
- Housework hours dropped sharply from 50 hours/week in 1920 to around 10 hours/week by 2019
- The steepest decline in housework hours occurred between 1920 and 1960
- There is an inverse relationship between appliance ownership and housework hours
