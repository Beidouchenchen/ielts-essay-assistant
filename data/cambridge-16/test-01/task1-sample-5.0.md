## Essay

The given two charts illustrates the percentage of households having electrical appliances and time spend on housework in one country from 1920 to 2019.

Overall, it is clearly seen that all three types of electrical appliances gradually increased over the years. On the other hand, the number of housework hours per week gradually decreased from 50 hours to 10 hours.

In 1920, 40% of households had washing machine, followed by vacuum cleaner with 30%. But, refrigerator was the least used appliance with no households using it. However, from 1920 to 1960, all three appliances gradually increased by approximately 20%. In addition, from 1960 to 2019, washing machine increased by 15% whereas refrigerator and vacuum cleaner increased by 70%.

In addition, the number of housework hours per week gradually decreased from 50 hours to 10 hours from 1920 to 2019. Furthermore, from 1920 to 1960, housework hours dropped by 32 hours. However, from 1960 to 2019, the number of housework hours decreased by only 8 hours.
