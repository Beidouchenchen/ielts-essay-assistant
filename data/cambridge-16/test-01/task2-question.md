# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 1

## Prompt

In some countries, more and more people are becoming interested in finding out about the history of the house or building they live in.

What are the reasons for this?

How can people research this?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Two-part question
- **Topic:** Interest in the history of houses/buildings
- **Parts:**
  1. Reasons why people are becoming interested in building history
  2. Methods people can use to research this
- **Key terms:** history, house/building, reasons, research
