## Essay

In some countries, people are highly concerned with knowing more about the history of the houses or buildings they live in. In this essay, I will discuss the main reasons why people want to know about their house's history and how they can research it.

First of all, one of the main reasons is that people want to understand more about their family roots. Some people are curious to know who lived in the house before them and what was the purpose of that building in the old times. For instance, people who live in historical buildings are always keen to know about its history.

Consequently, there are many ways to find out about the history of the house or building. One of the main ways is to consult with the older people of that area, as they have more knowledge about the history of the area and the buildings. In addition to this, people can also search on the internet, as there are many websites that provide information about historical buildings.

To conclude, people are interested in knowing about the history of their houses because of their curiosity about who lived there before them and the purpose of the building in the past. They can find out about it by consulting with older people or searching on the internet.

Cohesive devices are used well [*First of all | Consequently*] but there are some errors [*For this aims*].
