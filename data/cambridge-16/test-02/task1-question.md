# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 2

## Prompt

The diagram below shows the manufacturing process for making sugar from sugar cane.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### How sugar is produced from sugar cane

1. **Growing** (12–18 months) — Sugar canes are grown
2. **Harvesting** — Sugar canes are harvested by machine and by hand
3. **Crushing** — Sugar canes are crushed to extract juice
4. **Purifying juice** — Juice is filtered through a limestone filter
5. **Evaporator** — Juice becomes syrup through heating
6. **Centrifuge** — Separates sugar crystals from syrup
7. **Drying and cooling** — Final sugar product is dried and cooled

## Key Visual Features

- The process begins with growing sugar cane for 12-18 months
- Harvesting is done both mechanically and manually
- Crushing extracts the juice from the sugar canes
- The juice is purified using a limestone filter
- Heating in an evaporator turns juice into syrup
- A centrifuge separates sugar crystals from the syrup
- The final step involves drying and cooling to produce the finished sugar
- The process is linear with no branching or循环
