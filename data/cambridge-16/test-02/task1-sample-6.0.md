## Essay

The given diagram illustrates the process of producing sugar from sugar cane.

Overall, it is a seven-stage process which begins with growing sugar canes and ends with drying and cooling the sugar. The process includes harvesting, crushing, purifying, evaporating and centrifuging stages.

In the first stage, sugar canes are grown for 12 to 18 months. After that, sugar canes are harvested by both machine and manual labour. Next, sugar canes are crushed to extract juice. Then, the juice is purified through a limestone filter. After that, the juice is heated in an evaporator, which turns it into syrup. Subsequently, the syrup is put into a centrifuge, which separates sugar crystals from the syrup. Finally, the sugar crystals are dried and cooled to produce the final product.

The process is quite straightforward and involves mechanical and chemical stages to transform raw sugar cane into refined sugar.
