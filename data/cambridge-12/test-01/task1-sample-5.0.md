## Examiner's Comment
The candidate covers the key features and rounds off the description with an overview. Organisation is evident, however not wholly logical, and it is sometimes difficult to relate data to specific age groups. Vocabulary is just about adequate, but is rather dependent on input material [regular physical activity | percentage]: original material is limited to e.g. [teenager | mid-twenties] with a lot of inaccuracy in word formation. Most sentence forms consist of simple structures and these are often inaccurate [bars's changed and interesting | men's percentage are increasing | the percentage more decrease until], though there is some accuracy in comparative forms [is higher than].

## Strengths
- Covers key features with overview
- Some comparative structures used
- Adequate vocabulary for the task

## Weaknesses
- Organisation not wholly logical
- Difficult to relate data to specific age groups
- Dependent on input material vocabulary
- Frequent word formation inaccuracies
- Simple sentence structures predominantly inaccurate
- Spelling and grammatical errors throughout
