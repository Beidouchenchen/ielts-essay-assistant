# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 5

## Prompt
The bar chart below shows the percentage of Australian men and women in different age groups who did regular physical activity in 2010.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Age Group | Male (%) | Female (%) |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| 15 to 24 | 52.8 | 47.7 |
| 25 to 34 | 42.2 | 48.9 |
| 35 to 44 | 39.5 | 52.5 |
| 45 to 54 | 43.1 | 53.3 |
| 55 to 64 | 45.1 | 53.0 |
| 65 and over | 46.7 | 47.1 |

## Key Visual Features
- Grouped bar chart with male vs female for 6 age groups
- Male participation highest in 15-24 (52.8%), lowest in 35-44 (39.5%)
- Female participation highest in 55-64 (53.3%), lowest in 15-24 (47.7%)
- Males higher only in youngest group (15-24)
- Females higher in all groups from 25 onwards
- Gap narrows significantly in 65+ group
