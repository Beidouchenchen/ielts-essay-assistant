# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 6

## Prompt
At the present time, the population of some countries includes a relatively large number of young adults, compared with the number of older people.

Do the advantages of this situation outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** advantages outweigh disadvantages
- **Topic:** population demographics (youth bulge)
- **Required stance:** state whether advantages outweigh disadvantages
- **Requirements:** discuss both advantages and disadvantages; provide reasons and examples
