# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 6

## Prompt
The maps below show the centre of a small town called Islip as it is now, and plans for its development.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features
**Now:**
- Main road running east-west through centre
- Shops on both sides of main road
- Housing on south side
- Park on east side
- School on south-west
- Countryside to the north

**Planned development:**
- Dual carriageway replaces main road (curved, surrounding centre)
- Shopping centre replaces individual shops
- Bus station added (north-west)
- Car park added (north)
- Pedestrians only zone in centre
- New housing in two areas (north-east and south)
- Park remains but smaller
- School remains
- Countryside disappears
