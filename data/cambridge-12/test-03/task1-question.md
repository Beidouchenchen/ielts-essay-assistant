# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 7

## Prompt
The chart below shows how frequently people in the USA ate in fast food restaurants between 2003 and 2013.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Frequency | 2003 (%) | 2006 (%) | 2013 (%) |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Every day | ~3 | ~3 | ~3 |
| Several times a week | ~15 | ~20 | ~20 |
| Once a week | ~30 | ~33 | ~27 |
| Once or twice a month | ~30 | ~33 | ~33 |
| A few times a year | ~12 | ~15 | ~15 |
| Never | ~5 | ~3 | ~3 |

## Key Visual Features
- Grouped bar chart with 3 years (2003, 2006, 2013) across 6 frequency categories
- 'Once or twice a month' consistently most popular
- 'Once a week' peaked in 2006, declined by 2013
- 'Several times a week' increased over the period
- 'Every day' remained consistently low (~3%)
- 'Never' category decreased slightly
