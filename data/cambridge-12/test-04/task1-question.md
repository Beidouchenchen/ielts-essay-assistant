# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 8

## Prompt
The diagram below shows how geothermal energy is used to produce electricity.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features
**Geothermal power plant process (5 stages):**
1. Cold water stored in a tank at surface level
2. Water pumped down through injection well (~4.5km deep) to geothermal zone (hot rocks)
3. Water heated by hot rocks, rises through production well
4. Steam enters turbine, which powers generator; condensed water returns to condenser
5. Condenser cools steam back to water, recycled to injection well

**Labels:** Cold water, Injection well, Geothermal zone (hot rocks), Production well, Condenser, Turbine, Generator, 4.5km depth
