# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 15, Test 4

## Prompt

In some cultures, children are often told that they can achieve anything if they try hard enough.

What are the advantages and disadvantages of giving children this message?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Two-part question (advantages + disadvantages)
- **Topic:** Telling children they can achieve anything if they try hard
- **Parts:**
  1. What are the advantages of this message?
  2. What are the disadvantages of this message?
- **Key terms:** children, achieve, try hard, advantages, disadvantages
