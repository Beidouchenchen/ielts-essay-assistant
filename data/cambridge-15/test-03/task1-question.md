# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 15, Test 3

## Prompt

The diagram below shows how instant noodles are manufactured.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Manufacturing instant noodles

1. **Storage silos** — Flour is stored in large silos and delivered by truck
2. **Mixer** — Flour is mixed with water and oil in a mixer
3. **Dough sheets** — The mixture is formed into dough sheets through rollers
4. **Dough strips** — Dough sheets are cut into dough strips
5. **Noodles discs** — Strips are formed into round noodle discs
6. **Cooking (oil) + drying** — Noodles are cooked in oil and dried
7. **Vegetables + spices** — Vegetables and spices are added to the noodles
8. **Labelling + sealing** — Noodles are placed in cups, labelled, and sealed

## Key Visual Features

- The process is linear with 8 distinct stages
- Flour is the primary ingredient, delivered from storage silos
- Water and oil are added during the mixing stage
- The process involves both mechanical (rollers, cutting) and cooking steps
- Final product is packaged in cups with labels
- The entire process transforms raw flour into packaged instant noodles
