# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 15, Test 2

## Prompt

The graph below shows the number of tourists visiting a particular Caribbean island between 2010 and 2017.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Number of tourists visiting a Caribbean island (2010–2017)

| Year | Total visitors (millions) | Visitors staying on cruise ships | Visitors staying on island |
|------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------|
| 2010 | 1.0 | 0.25 | 0.75 |
| 2011 | 1.2 | 0.3 | 0.9 |
| 2012 | 1.5 | 0.4 | 1.1 |
| 2013 | 2.0 | 0.5 | 1.5 |
| 2014 | 2.5 | 0.75 | 1.75 |
| 2015 | 2.7 | 1.0 | 1.7 |
| 2016 | 2.7 | 1.2 | 1.5 |
| 2017 | 3.5 | 2.0 | 1.5 |

## Key Visual Features

- Total visitors grew steadily from 1M to 3.5M over the period
- Cruise ship visitors showed the most dramatic increase (0.25M → 2.0M)
- Island-staying visitors grew initially but plateaued around 1.5M after 2013
- From 2013 onward, cruise ship visitors grew faster than island-staying visitors
- By 2017, cruise ship visitors exceeded island-staying visitors for the first time
