## Essay

Nowadays people prefer to read news on the Internet. Besides, children's won't read hundreds of fictional e-books on devices like Amazon's Kindle or others. The reason makes people believe that no one will purchase printed newspapers or books and people will read everything online without any payment.

Electronic books and newspapers have many advantages. They are easy to carry and read. They can be stored in computers, mobiles or e-readers and in many other formats and are available at any time. Moreover, people who are interested in buying can purchase them online.

Digital books and newspapers also have some important advantages – they are environmentally friendly. For example, many trees can be saved if people don't use paper versions of books or news. Additionally, the production of paper and print affects the environment. All of these facts could convince more people to choose digital versions of books and newspapers.

On the other hand, traditional printed books and newspapers have much to offer. They have created a sort of emotional connection and value for people. When a reader sits in front of a fire with a book in his hands, it creates a special atmosphere. We cannot ignore the beauty of art, statues and paintings. You can find a piece of fine arts' printing, but the printing of digital books on the screen can never be as attractive as the printed version.

Based on this, I believe that the amount of printed newspapers will decrease considerably and simply disappear while traditional books will always be popular. We can see many examples that show how special and emotional printed books are, although many people like and value an addition to the things we just written about.

I don't think, though, there is a big chance magazines and newspapers will exist on the paper, especially as people like to read news on the Internet or on e-readers and no one needs extra and unnecessary wastes. Printing so a huge amount of articles will demand additional resources and make them less competitive even in terms of price.
