# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 15, Test 1

## Prompt

The chart below shows the results of a survey about people's coffee and tea buying and drinking habits in five Australian cities.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Coffee and tea buying and drinking habits in five cities in Australia

| City | Bought fresh coffee (last 4 weeks) | Bought instant coffee (last 4 weeks) | Went to a cafe for coffee or tea (last 4 weeks) |
|------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Sydney | 45% | 35% | 65% |
| Melbourne | 42% | 30% | 65% |
| Brisbane | 35% | 45% | 55% |
| Adelaide | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Hobart | 30% | 45% | 60% |

## Key Visual Features

- Sydney and Melbourne have the highest cafe usage (both ~65%)
- Brisbane has the highest instant coffee purchases (~45%)
- Adelaide has the lowest fresh coffee purchases (~30%)
- Hobart shows high instant coffee and cafe usage
- Fresh coffee purchases are relatively similar across cities (30-45%)
- Cafe visits are generally popular across all five cities
