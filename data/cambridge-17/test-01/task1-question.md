# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 1

## Prompt

The maps below show an industrial area in the town of Norbiton, and planned future development of the site.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Norbiton Industrial Area - Now

- Factory buildings in centre
- Roads connecting buildings
- Farmland to north
- River separating farmland from industrial area
- Main road at south

### Norbiton Industrial Area - Planned Future Development

- Housing replaces factories
- School and playground added (east side)
- Shops and medical centre added (around roundabout)
- Bridge planned across river
- Access to farmland housing

## Key Visual Features

- **Map comparison** (current vs planned)
- Factories → Housing
- New bridge across river
- School and playground added
- Shops and medical centre added
