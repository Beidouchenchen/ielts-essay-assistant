# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 2

## Prompt

The bar chart below shows the percentage of people in the UK who watched television programmes in different genres in 2010 and 2018.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### TV Programme Viewing by Genre in UK (%)

| Genre | 2010 | 2018 |
|-------|------|------|
| Drama | 45% | 52% |
| Comedy | 38% | 42% |
| Reality | 32% | 48% |
| News | 55% | 45% |
| Sports | 28% | 35% |
| Documentary | 20% | 18% |

## Key Visual Features

- **Bar chart** comparing 6 genres across 2 years
- Reality shows biggest increase (32% → 48%)
- News decreased significantly (55% → 45%)
- Drama and Comedy increased moderately
- Sports increased, Documentary decreased slightly
