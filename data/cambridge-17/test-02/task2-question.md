# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 2

## Prompt

Some children spend hours every day on their smartphones.

Why is this the case? Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Opinion essay (positive or negative) + explanation
- **Topic:** Children spending time on smartphones
- **Part 1:** Why is this the case? (reasons)
- **Part 2:** Positive or negative development? (opinion)
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
