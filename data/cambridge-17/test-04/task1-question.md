# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 4

## Prompt

The line graph below shows the number of shops that closed and the number of new shops that opened in a particular country between 2011 and 2018.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Shop Closures and Openings (2011-2018)

| Year | Closures | Openings |
|------|----------|----------|
| 2011 | ~6,400 | ~8,500 |
| 2012 | ~5,000 | ~4,000 |
| 2013 | ~5,200 | ~5,500 |
| 2014 | ~5,500 | ~6,000 |
| 2015 | ~700 | ~5,500 |
| 2016 | ~5,000 | ~3,500 |
| 2017 | ~5,100 | ~3,400 |
| 2018 | ~5,000 | ~3,000 |

## Key Visual Features

- **Line graph** with two lines (closures and openings) over 8 years
- Closures: fluctuated around 5,000-6,400, dramatic fall in 2015 to ~700
- Openings: started high (~8,500), declined overall to ~3,000
- Openings generally higher than closures except 2015
- Overall downward trend in both metrics
