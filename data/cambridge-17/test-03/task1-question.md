# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 3

## Prompt

The charts below show the main land use in the Chorleywood area in 1965 and at present.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Land Use in Chorleywood Area

| Land Use | 1965 | Present |
|----------|------|---------|
| Housing | 40% | 55% |
| Parkland | 25% | 20% |
| Agriculture | 20% | 15% |
| Woodland | 10% | 5% |
| Commercial | 5% | 5% |

## Key Visual Features

- **Pie charts** comparing land use 1965 vs present
- Housing increased significantly (40% → 55%)
- Parkland decreased slightly (25% → 20%)
- Agriculture decreased (20% → 15%)
- Woodland halved (10% → 5%)
- Commercial remained stable (5%)
