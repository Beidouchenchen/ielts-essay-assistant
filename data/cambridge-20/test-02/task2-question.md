# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 20, Test 2

## Prompt

In many countries, primary and secondary schools close for two months or more in the summer holidays.

What is the value of long school holidays?

What are the arguments in favour of shorter school holidays?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Discussion essay (two-part question)
- **Topic:** Education / school holidays
- **Part 1:** Value of long school holidays
- **Part 2:** Arguments for shorter school holidays
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
- **Note:** Must address BOTH parts equally
