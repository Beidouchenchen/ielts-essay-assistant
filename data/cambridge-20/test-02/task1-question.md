# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 20, Test 2

## Prompt

The plans below show the site of a farm in 1950 and the same site today.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features

- **Map comparison** showing before/after of the same site
- **Track → Road**: accessibility improved
- **Agricultural → Tourism**: farm shop, camping field, holiday cottages added
- **Barn replaced** by holiday cottages near the river
- **Two parking zones** added near the road
- **Solar panels** installed for power generation
- **Fruit trees, vegetables, chickens retained** but with new tourism facilities alongside
