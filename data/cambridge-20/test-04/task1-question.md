# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 20, Test 4

## Prompt

The diagram below shows how fabric is manufactured from bamboo.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### How Bamboo Fabric is Made

| Stage | Action | Details |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Plant bamboo plants | Spring |
| 2 | Harvest | Autumn |
| 3 | Cut into strips | Bamboo stems cut into long strips |
| 4 | Crush strips | To make liquid pulp |
| 5 | Filter | Separate long fibres from liquid |
| 6 | Soften fibres | Add water and amine oxide |
| 7 | Spin | To make yarn |
| 8 | Weave | To make fabric |

## Key Visual Features

- **Linear process diagram** with 8 sequential stages
- **Seasonal timing**: plant in spring, harvest in autumn
- **Physical processing**: cutting, crushing, filtering, softening
- **Chemical treatment**: water and amine oxide for softening
- **Textile production**: spinning into yarn, weaving into fabric
- **End product**: fabric (shown as clothing)
