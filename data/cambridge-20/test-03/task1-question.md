# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 20, Test 3

## Prompt

The charts below give information about a public library in a town called Little Chalfont.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Little Chalfont Library

| Age Group | Percentage |
|-----------|-----------|
| Children | 22% |
| Young adults 13-17 | Smallest group |
| Adults 18-64 | 51% |
| Adults 65 and over | ~25% |

## Key Visual Features

- **Three different chart types** combined in one task
- **Pie chart**: majority of members are adults 18-64 (51%)
- **Table**: fiction is the most popular category (38% each for children and adults)
- **Bar chart**: overall upward trend in loans from 2007 to 2016
- **Dip in loans** around 2012-2013 before recovery
