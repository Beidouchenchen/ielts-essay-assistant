## Essay

Most contemporary economies allow for a model of employment along with being self-employed, which appears very tempting for many. However, still vast majority of people opt for being employed by a company, although in recent years this number has steadily been decreasing. This essay will look at the benefits of choosing to be self-employed.

The main benefit of being self-employed is the ability to choose what and when you work. There is a vast array of business ventures and undertaking available. They range from highly specialised services or products, where only a few people may be skilled or trained, to more mundane jobs, which are really only suited for those who are less able and willing. And while it is true that, as a business owner, the buck stops with you, the benefits of being your own boss can far outweigh any disadvantages. It is true that self-employment has its risks, but if there are two breadwinners in the household with no dependants, and if both can be flexible about the type of work they do, this risk can be significantly reduced. It is also true that self-employment can be financially rewarding. Working for yourself can be an opportunity to have a higher income than as a waged worker, although it depends a lot on the type of work.

The first reason relates to lifestyle. In Australia and many other countries, many young people are embracing the idea of being self-employed as it offers more freedom. In the world of social media and the Internet, many young people embrace the opportunity to express their thoughts, dreams and aspirations, and earning a living on line offers that. Those who have a business or are self-employed, have more freedom to spend time with their family and friends, to travel and generally to enjoy a better lifestyle. This is obviously not possible if you are working regular hours for an employer.

Apparently the second issue is that people might anticipate an unpleasant environment in the meantime to the pace and bustle of working life. Self-employment has a number of disadvantages. It may be harder and those people who have left it for the moment might not have the luxury or the flexibility which they would have desired. People need to consider their financial situation if they are going to be self-employed, and this can bring additional stress into their lives, particularly during the early stages of setting up a business.

In conclusion, being self-employed has its drawbacks, which is why many people choose to be employed.
