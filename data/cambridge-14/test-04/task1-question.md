# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 14, Test 4

## Prompt

The plans below show a public park when it first opened in 1920 and the same park today.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Map Description

### Grange Park (1920)
- **Entrances**: Arnold Avenue (north), Eldon Street (south)
- **Features**:
  - Rose garden (northwest corner)
  - Stage for musicians (west centre)
  - Fountain (centre)
  - Rose garden (southeast corner)
  - Glasshouse (south)
  - Pond for water plants (east)
  - Seats around perimeter

### Grange Park (today)
- **Entrances**: Arnold Avenue (north), Eldon Street (south), new entrance to underground car park (southeast)
- **Features**:
  - Rose garden (northwest, reduced size)
  - Amphitheatre for concerts (west, replaced stage)
  - Central rose garden with surrounding seats (replaced fountain)
  - Cafe (northeast, replaced rose garden)
  - Children's play area (east)
  - Water feature (south, replaced pond)
  - Glasshouse removed
  - Underground car park added

## Key Visual Features

- **Map comparison** (1920 vs today)
- **Major changes**: fountain → central rose garden, stage → amphitheatre, pond → water feature
- **New additions**: cafe, children's play area, underground car park
- **Removed**: glasshouse
- **Two entrances** remain, one new car park entrance added
