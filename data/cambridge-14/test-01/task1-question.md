# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 14, Test 1

## Prompt

The charts below show the average percentages in typical meals of three types of nutrients, all of which may be unhealthy if eaten too much.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Average percentages of sodium, saturated fats and added sugars in typical meals consumed in the USA

| Meal | Sodium | Saturated fat | Added sugar |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Breakfast | 14% | 16% | 16% |
| Lunch | 29% | 26% | 19% |
| Dinner | 43% | 37% | 23% |
| Snacks | 14% | 21% | 42% |

## Key Visual Features

- **Three pie charts** showing distribution of nutrients across meals
- **Same four meal categories** in each chart: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks
- **Sodium**: Dinner dominates (43%), Lunch second (29%), Breakfast and Snacks equal (14%)
- **Saturated fat**: Dinner highest (37%), Lunch second (26%), Snacks (21%), Breakfast lowest (16%)
- **Added sugar**: Snacks dominate (42%), Dinner second (23%), Lunch (19%), Breakfast lowest (16%)
- **Dinner** is highest for sodium and saturated fat
- **Snacks** is highest for added sugar
- **Breakfast** consistently lowest across all three nutrients
