## Essay

A hydroelectric power station can generate electricity power by using the energy of water flowing from high level to low level. It works different functions in a day and night.

During the day, the power station uses water flow from the high-level reservoir to generate electricity. The intake is open only during the day time. Then the water flows down the river and it is possible to see a generator. The generator is connected to power lines, which is used to transmit the electricity generated from the generator. The generated electricity then goes to the national grid and then to people's homes.

On the other hand, at the night time, the power station works in a different way. At night, it pumps water from the low-level reservoir to high-level reservoir to provide the enough water for day time's work. The intake is closed during the night time.

In a conclusion, a hydroelectric power station can provide electricity power at the different nights of the day. The power station works in a different way in day and night time to generate and store the energy for the day's work.
