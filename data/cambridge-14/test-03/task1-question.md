# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 14, Test 3

## Prompt

The diagram below shows how electricity is generated in a hydroelectric power station.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Diagram Description

The hydroelectric power station operates on a day and night cycle:

**Daytime process:**
1. River feeds into high-level reservoir (held by dam)
2. Intake opens during the day
3. Water flows from high-level reservoir through the power station
4. Flow of water drives the generator
5. Generator produces electricity sent to national grid via power lines
6. Water collects in low-level reservoir

**Nighttime process:**
1. Intake closes at night
2. Reversible turbines switch to pumping mode
3. Water is pumped from low-level reservoir back to high-level reservoir
4. Cycle repeats the next day

## Key Visual Features

- **Process diagram** with day/night cycle
- **Two-stage operation**: generation (day) and pumping (night)
- **Key components**: dam, high-level reservoir, intake, power station, generator, reversible turbines, low-level reservoir, power lines, national grid
- **Reversible turbines** are the central mechanism
- **Closed-loop water system** between two reservoirs
