# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 14, Test 2

## Prompt

The chart below shows the value of one country's exports in various categories during 2015 and 2016. The table shows the percentage change in each category of exports in 2016 compared with 2015.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Export Earnings (2015-2016)

| Category | 2015 ($ billions) | 2016 ($ billions) |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Petroleum products | ~60 | ~62 |
| Engineered goods | ~55 | ~58 |
| Gems and jewellery | ~42 | ~40 |
| Agricultural products | ~32 | ~33 |
| Textiles | ~26 | ~30 |

### Percentage change in values (2015-2016)

| Category | Change |
|----------|--------|
| Petroleum products | ↑ 3% |
| Engineered goods | ↑ 8.5% |
| Gems and jewellery | ↓ 5.18% |
| Agricultural products | ↑ 0.81% |
| Textiles | ↑ 15.24% |

## Key Visual Features

- **Mixed chart**: bar chart + table
- **5 export categories** compared across two years
- **Petroleum products** highest in absolute value
- **Textiles** shows largest percentage growth (15.24%)
- **Gems and jewellery** only category that decreased
- **Agricultural products** smallest percentage change (0.81%)
