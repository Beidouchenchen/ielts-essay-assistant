# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 14, Test 2

## Prompt

Some people say that the main environmental problem of our time is the loss of particular species of plants and animals. Others say that there are more important environmental problems.

Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Discussion essay (discuss both views + give opinion)
- **Topic:** Environmental problems - species loss vs other issues
- **Views:**
  1. Loss of particular species is the main environmental problem
  2. There are more important environmental problems
- **Required stance:** Discuss both views and give own opinion
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
