# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 4

## Prompt

The charts below give information on the location and types of dance classes young people in a town in Australia are currently attending.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Location of Dance Classes (Pie Chart)

| Location | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Private studios | 48% |
| School halls (after-school) | 24% |
| Community halls & other | 18% |
| College-based studios | 10% |

### Types of Dance Classes by Age Group (Bar Chart)

| Dance Type | Under 11 | 11-16 |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Ballet | ~600 | ~300 |
| Tap | ~450 | ~420 |
| Modern | ~300 | ~515 |

## Key Visual Features

- **Two chart types**: pie chart + bar chart
- **Pie chart**: Private studios dominate (48%), nearly half
- **Bar chart**: Ballet most popular for Under 11 (~600 students)
- **Bar chart**: Modern most popular for 11-16 (~515 students)
- **Tap** relatively popular for both age groups
- **Under 11** prefer Ballet, **11-16** prefer Modern
