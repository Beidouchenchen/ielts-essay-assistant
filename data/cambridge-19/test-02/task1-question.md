# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 2

## Prompt

The plans below show a harbour in 2000 and how it looks today.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

| Road | Runs through middle |

## Key Visual Features

- **Map comparison** showing before/after of harbour
- **Marina moved** and replaced by fishing boats
- **New facilities**: second car park, second showers/toilets, cafés & shops
- **Castle demolished** → Hotel built
- **Public beach → Private beach** for hotel
- **Dock and ferries retained**
