# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 2

## Prompt

The working week should be shorter and workers should have a longer weekend.

Do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Opinion essay (agree or disagree)
- **Topic:** Working hours / work-life balance
- **Key claim:** Working week should be shorter + longer weekend
- **Required stance:** Agree or disagree (must choose one)
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
