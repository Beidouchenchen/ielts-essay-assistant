## Essay

I personally agree that working week should be shorter as my point of view on this topic is that workers should be given more time to spend with their families.

First and foremost, (IND) Many labours across the globe are exhausted by working five days a week and a six-hour shift is quite demanding, especially for those who have other important things to attend to like family matters. This would surely contribute to the overall well-being of workers as they would even enjoy their time together, like what if workers had a long weekend, like a Saturday to enjoy themselves.

Secondly, I would like to focus on leisure activities in El Salvador and during the holidays. For example, if a day off is given to a worker, he or she could just relax at home and spend time with their family. This will reduce the stress levels for workers like me, and will surely enhance their productivity at work. What I mean, I have a friend who always feels exhausted after working five days, and on weekends she goes to the park and feels better and more relaxed. And it could help us to enjoy things like sports or other activities. What if there are other activities like going out to have a picnic, you will feel and will be able to be a more productive worker and spend quality time with your family.

Though I also agree that a shorter working week might be good, it could lead to some economic issues. However, if the government made a compulsory plan for workers to have more holidays, for example, giving four days to work and making three days off, people would feel less stressed and enjoy more with their family.

Here is the examiner's comment:

This is a good response. The candidate provides their opinion at the start. They give ideas for supporting a shorter working week, and the discussion is easy to follow. However, a counter argument is included but the development and illustration of this could be stronger, as the example given (four working days with three days off) is presented as an additional idea.

Overall, ideas are presented in a clearer order, with some good use of linking vocabulary and cohesive devices [First and foremost | For example | What I mean | However]. There are attempts to use less common vocabulary [exhausted | compulsory | enhanced], and the candidate shows some flexibility in using vocabulary. There is a range of structures including conditionals [if] and multi-clause sentences. There are some errors in prepositions and other structures [I am used to do / I am used to doing], but the impact is not disruptive.

To achieve a higher score, ideas could be more fully extended, for example, by giving more detail on how removing a working day could be negative for the economy or for workers.
