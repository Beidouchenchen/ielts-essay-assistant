## Essay

The given diagram explains the way in which ethanol, a type of biofuel, is produced. Overall, there are four main phases in the production process, starting with growth (with both sunlight and carbon dioxide needed for this phase), harvesting, processing and, finally, consumption.

During the first step in the process of producing ethanol, plants and trees are grown, thanks to the energy of the sun and the carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. Then, these plants and trees are harvested with the help of a tractor. After harvesting, there is a pre-processing phase, in which the raw materials are processed to obtain cellulose.

In the next stage, the cellulose is taken to a factory, in which it is processed to extract sugars. This is a key stage in the process, as the sugars will be fermented using yeasts and microbes to produce the ethanol. The ethanol is then used as a fuel to power different types of vehicle, such as cars, vans and trucks.
