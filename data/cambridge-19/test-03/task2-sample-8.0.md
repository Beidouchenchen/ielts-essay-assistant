# Task 2 Sample Answer - Band 8.0

**Score:** 8.0
**Quality:** excellent

## Essay

Saving money is a topic that almost everybody has an opinion about. While some people think that saving money is not important as we cannot know what will happen tomorrow, others firmly believe that we should always set aside some money for our future. As far as I am concerned, I believe that saving is a good thing, and it applies to all ages.

First of all, some people are actually against the idea of saving money. They think that saving money is a waste of time because who knows what will happen tomorrow. Some people argue that if a person saves money and then dies, he or she will not be able to enjoy the fruits of their labour, so they prefer to live in the present and not set aside money. Another point of view regarding the topic of saving money is that young people should be saving for their future as they have a long time ahead of them, and when they get old they will not be able to work and earn money and their savings would be really useful for them.

However, there are a lot of things that should be considered when it comes to saving. For instance, unforeseen expenses or emergencies, like health problems, will always be around the corner. For instance, my mother always said that young people should set money aside for a rainy day and she was right, as we were able to pay for my university studies. That is why, I believe that young people especially should save money for their future. All in all, in my opinion saving money is a good thing and it is better to be safe than sorry.

## Examiner's Comment

This is a strong response to the task. In the first paragraph, the candidate presents both views on the topic, and makes it clear that they think saving money is a good idea. The main body paragraphs describe how saving money is important, and this point is supported with good examples. The conclusion summarises the main ideas.

Overall, ideas are easy to follow and logically organised. Linking expressions are well integrated [Whether | Sometimes | Perhaps] and generally accurate, and cohesive devices are used well [Many tend to | everybody who] with some errors [own choices / their own choices]. Paragraphing at the start is appropriate, but the last four sentences are set out separately and inappropriately, which affects the score.

There is some higher-level vocabulary, with effective collocation [full of enthusiasm | tend to live in the present | settle down | unforeseen expenses]. The candidate uses a variety of structures, including comparative forms [rather than | do you prefer], conditionals [If] and a range of multi-clause sentences. Errors remain, with some missing punctuation and incorrect tenses [be found out / find out] and prepositions [In any age / At any age], but this does not affect the reader's understanding.

To achieve a higher score, the candidate should ensure that the conclusion summarises the main ideas, and use effective paragraphing, especially in the second half of the response.

## Strengths
- Clear presentation of both views
- Strong personal examples (mother's advice, university studies)
- Well-integrated linking expressions
- Higher-level vocabulary with effective collocation
- Variety of structures including comparatives and conditionals
- Clear position throughout

## Weaknesses
- Inappropriate paragraphing in second half (last 4 sentences separate)
- Some errors in prepositions [In any age / At any age]
- Some missing punctuation
- Tense errors [be found out / find out]
- Conclusion could better summarise main ideas
