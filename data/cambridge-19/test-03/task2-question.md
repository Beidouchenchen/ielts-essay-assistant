# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 3

## Prompt

It is important for everyone, including young people, to save money for their future.

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Opinion essay (to what extent agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** Saving money / financial planning
- **Key claim:** Everyone (including young people) should save money
- **Required stance:** To what extent agree or disagree
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
- **Note:** "To what extent" allows for partial agreement
