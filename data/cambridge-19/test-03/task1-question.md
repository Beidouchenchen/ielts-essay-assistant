# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 3

## Prompt

The diagram below shows how a biofuel called ethanol is produced.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Biofuel Production: How Ethanol is Made

| Stage | Action | Details |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Plants and trees grow | Using energy from sunlight + carbon dioxide |
| 2 | Harvesting | Crops harvested by tractor |
| 3 | Pre-processing | Raw materials processed |
| 4 | Cellulose | Extracted from harvested crops |
| 5 | Processing | Cellulose processed in factory |
| 6 | Sugars | Extracted from processed cellulose |
| 7 | Microbes added | Added to sugars |
| 8 | Ethanol | Final product, used as fuel |

### Carbon Dioxide Cycle

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Plants absorb CO2 | During growth |
| Ethanol combustion | Cars, trucks, planes release CO2 |
| CO2 reabsorbed | By new plants (cycle continues) |

## Key Visual Features

- **Cyclical process diagram** with CO2 recycling
- **8 main stages** from plant growth to ethanol production
- **Carbon dioxide cycle**: plants absorb CO2 → ethanol made → CO2 released → plants absorb again
- **End uses**: cars, trucks, planes use ethanol fuel
- **Industrial processing**: factory involved in cellulose → sugars stage
