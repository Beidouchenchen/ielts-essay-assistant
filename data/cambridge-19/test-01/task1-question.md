# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 19, Test 1

## Prompt

The graph below gives information on the numbers of participants for different activities at one social centre in Melbourne, Australia for the period 2000 to 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Number of participants, by activity 2000-2020

| Year | Film club | Martial arts | Amateur dramatics | Table tennis | Musical performances |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| 2000 | 65 | 35 | 27 | 17 | 0 |
| 2005 | 62 | 35 | 27 | 20 | 0 |
| 2010 | 58 | 38 | 18 | 28 | 12 |
| 2015 | 63 | 37 | 12 | 35 | 15 |
| 2020 | 65 | 37 | 7 | 55 | 18 |

## Key Visual Features

- **Line graph** with 5 activities over 20 years
- **Film club** consistently highest, slight dip then recovery to 65
- **Table tennis** steady growth from 17 to 55 (largest increase)
- **Martial arts** relatively stable around 35-38
- **Amateur dramatics** steady decline from 27 to 7 (largest decrease)
- **Musical performances** started at 0 in 2000, grew to 18 by 2020
