## Essay

The given line graph illustrates data on the participant numbers for five activities at a social club in Melbourne, Australia for the first two decades of this century.

Overall, five activities are illustrated in the graph: film club, table tennis, martial arts, amateur dramatics and musical performances. It is clear that film club had the highest number of participants throughout the period, while musical performances had the fewest participants for most of the period.

Looking first at the most popular activity, film club, there were about 65 participants in 2000. Despite falling to around 58 in 2010, the number of participants rose to reach the same figure of 65 by 2020, making it the most popular activity in that year. However, there was a noticeable fall in the number of people attending amateur dramatics from 2000 to 2020, with about 27 attendees in 2000, followed by a steady decline to around 7 participants by 2020.

Regarding the remaining activities, there was a dramatic rise in the number of participants in table tennis, from below 20 in 2000 to about 55 in 2020, overtaking martial arts at some point during the period. There was little change in the popularity of martial arts, which was attended by about 35 participants for most of the period, although there was a slight rise to about 38 in 2010. Finally, there were no participants in musical performances in 2000, but this number rose to about 20 participants in 2020.

## Examiner's Comment

This good response begins with an introduction, then in the second paragraph the writer gives an overview of the changes in the participant numbers for the five activities at the social centre in Melbourne. This information is organised logically and a range of cohesive devices and vocabulary are used well [Despite falling | looking first at | regarding]. There is some flexible reference and paraphrasing [which was attended by | this number rose to].

Vocabulary is generally good [involved in | dramatic fall | steady decline | overtaking], with some higher-level vocabulary [illustrates | decade | attendee]. There is a variety of grammatical structures, including comparative forms and conditional clauses. There are some errors in prepositions and other structures [with about 27 participants in 2000, followed by] but the impact is not disruptive.

To achieve a higher score, the overview could include a sum-up of the most significant changes and this could be followed by a conclusion, or the overview could refer to the data about the activities which showed a downward trend, e.g. the participant numbers for amateur dramatics and for film club fell to about 2010 and 2015, before increasing again. The candidate should also demonstrate a greater level of accuracy with grammatical structures.
