## Essay

Competition is a really big thing, why? Because it is kind of a push of working in our life. For instance, the good thing to have competition in our life is to strive hard in order for us to achieve our goals and aims. But in school also competition should be part of our daily lives. For example, when we are in school we need to compete for our grades, right? But the thing is, for-profit, the competition must be healthy. There are benefits of competition in all spheres of life, whether in school, work or personal life.

First, working or having a competition at work will be able to help us to have a better life. For example, if I am working in a restaurant and my boss told me to work harder, I would surely work hard and be competitive to do well in my position, so that I would not be replaced. This is a good way to strive harder in life. We should always consider that competition is part of our lives, and we should embrace it.

In school, when we are having a test in school, we strive hard to strive for it, to pass it. For example, if I do not study well, I might fail in all of my subjects and it is a bad thing to feel. Furthermore, when we are having a test we are trying hard to pass it and become a good student. That is why, we should always think that competition is part of our life, whether in school or in personal life.

Life itself is not a competition, it is a journey.

## Examiner's Comment

This is an adequate response to the task. The main ideas are presented in a logical order, and the response is organised into paragraphs. The candidate attempts to use a range of cohesive devices, but there is some repetition [competition | strive]. There is an attempt to use a range of vocabulary, with some less common items [embrace | sphere], but there are errors in word choice [a push of working | for-profit | strive for it] and spelling [surely]. The range of grammatical structures is limited, with some error-free sentences, but also errors in subject-verb agreement, articles and prepositions.

To achieve a higher score, the candidate could fully extend their ideas, develop the main points more fully, and demonstrate a greater level of accuracy with vocabulary and grammatical structures.
