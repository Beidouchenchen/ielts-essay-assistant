# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 1

## Prompt
The first chart below shows how energy is used in an average Australian household. The second chart shows the greenhouse gas emissions which result from this energy use.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Australian household energy use
| Category | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Heating | 42% |
| Water heating | 30% |
| Other appliances | 15% |
| Refrigeration | 7% |
| Lighting | 4% |
| Cooling | 2% |

### Australian household greenhouse gas emissions
| Category | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Water heating | 32% |
| Other appliances | 28% |
| Heating | 15% |
| Refrigeration | 14% |
| Lighting | 8% |
| Cooling | 3% |

## Key Visual Features
- Two pie charts comparing energy use and greenhouse gas emissions
- Heating is the largest energy consumer (42%) but produces only 15% of emissions
- Water heating is the largest emission source (32%) from 30% energy use
- Other appliances produce disproportionately high emissions (28%) from limited energy use (15%)
- Refrigeration also produces higher emissions (14%) relative to energy use (7%)
