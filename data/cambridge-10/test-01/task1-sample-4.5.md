# Writing Task 1 Sample Answer
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 1
**Band:** 4.5

## Examiner's Comment
The response generally addresses the requirements of the task but there is no clear overview. Key features of the two charts are presented but not adequately highlighted. The main trend in the data is not clear. The message is difficult to follow, and there is no clear progression throughout. Organisation is evident, but the logical sequencing of information is weak. A limited range of cohesive devices is used and referencing is not always clear. An adequate vocabulary is used, although there are errors in word choice and spelling. Simple and compound sentence forms are used, although errors are frequent and may cause severe strain for the reader. There are also some errors in punctuation.

## Essay
The charts show how energy is used in an average Australian household and what amounts of greenhouse gases are produced from electricity usage, from different appliances.

Water heating is by far the largest consumer of energy, producing 30% of greenhouse gases, followed by heating, producing 42%. The other appliances are lighting, cooling, refrigeration and other appliances, which use 15%, 7%, 4% and 2% respectively. The greenhouse gases produced by these are 15%, 14%, 8% and 3%.

The second chart shows the greenhouse gases that result from this energy use. Water heating produces 32% of greenhouse gases, followed by other appliances at 28%. Heating produces 15%, refrigeration 14%, lighting 8% and cooling 3%.

## Strengths
- Addresses the task generally
- Some vocabulary related to the topic
- Basic sentence structures attempted

## Weaknesses
- No clear overview or main trend identified
- Key features not adequately highlighted
- Information not logically sequenced
- Limited cohesive devices
- Errors in word choice, spelling, and punctuation
- Severe strain on reader understanding
