# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 4

## Prompt
Many museums charge for admission while others are free.

Do you think the advantages of charging people for admission to museums outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** opinion (advantages outweigh disadvantages)
- **Topic:** museum admission fees
- **Required stance:** state whether advantages outweigh disadvantages
- **Requirements:** provide reasons and examples; consider pros and cons of charging admission
