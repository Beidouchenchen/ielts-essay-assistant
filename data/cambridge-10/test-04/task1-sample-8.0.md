# Writing Task 1 Sample Answer
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 4
**Band:** 8.0 (Model Answer)

## Essay
Salmon begin life as eggs on a pebbly riverbed, hidden from predators in reeds in slow-moving upper rivers. After five to six months the eggs hatch into 'fry' and move to faster-flowing waters in the lower reaches of the river. During this time they measure between 3 and 8 centimetres in length.

By the time salmon reach 12 to 15cm, they become 'smolt', and live near the river mouth. After they have spent three years at sea, the salmon will have then grown to adult size, which is between seventy and seventy-six centimetres in length. When they are fully-grown adults, the salmon travel back up river to spawn, and lay their eggs, and the cycle starts anew.

In summary, the salmon passes through three distinct physical stages as it grows to maturity, each of which is spent in a different environment. The first two stages are spent in a freshwater environment, while the final stage is feed in saltwater.

## Strengths
- Clear overview identifying three stages and environments
- Accurate data reporting with appropriate time markers
- Effective use of cohesive devices (by the time, after, in summary)
- Wide range of vocabulary (pebbly riverbed, predators, spawn)
- Varied sentence structures throughout
- Well-organised by life stage

## Weaknesses
- Could include more specific details about the environment
- The summary could be slightly more detailed
