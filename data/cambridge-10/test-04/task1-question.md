# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 4

## Prompt
The diagrams below show the life cycle of a species of large fish called the salmon.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Salmon Life Cycle
| Stage | Location | Duration | Size |
|-------|----------|----------|------|
| 1. Eggs | Upper river (slow moving) among reeds and small stones | Approx 3-6 months | - |
| 2. Fry | Lower river (fast flowing) | Approx 4 years | 3-8 cm |
| 3. Smolt | Open sea | Approx 3 years | 12-15 cm |
| 4. Adult salmon | Return to upper river | - | 70-76 cm |

## Key Visual Features
- Process diagram showing salmon life cycle
- Three main stages: fry (3-8cm), smolt (12-15cm), adult salmon (70-76cm)
- Eggs laid in upper river (slow moving) among reeds and small stones
- Fry develop in lower river (fast flowing) for approximately 4 years
- Smolt move to open sea for approximately 3 years
- Adult salmon return to upper river to lay eggs, completing the cycle
