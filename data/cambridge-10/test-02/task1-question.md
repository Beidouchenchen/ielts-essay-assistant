# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 2

## Prompt
The tables below give information about sales of Fairtrade-labelled coffee and bananas in 1999 and 2004 in five European countries.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Coffee sales (millions of euros)
| Country | 1999 | 2004 |
|---------|------|------|
| UK | 1.5 | 20 |
| Switzerland | 3 | 6 |
| Denmark | 1.8 | 2 |
| Belgium | 1 | 1.7 |
| Sweden | 0.8 | 1 |

### Banana sales (millions of euros)
| Country | 1999 | 2004 |
|---------|------|------|
| Switzerland | 15 | 47 |
| UK | 1 | 5.5 |
| Belgium | 0.6 | 4 |
| Sweden | 1.8 | 1 |
| Denmark | 2 | 0.9 |

## Key Visual Features
- Two tables comparing Fairtrade coffee and banana sales across 5 countries
- Coffee sales increased in all countries from 1999 to 2004
- UK coffee sales showed the most dramatic increase (1.5 to 20 million euros)
- Switzerland had the highest banana sales in both years (15 to 47 million euros)
- Banana sales decreased in Sweden and Denmark
