# Writing Task 1 Sample Answer
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 2
**Band:** 7.5 (Model Answer)

## Essay
The two tables contain sales data for Fairtrade coffee and bananas in 1999 and 2004, in five European nations.

Coffee sales, in all five countries, increased, albeit to widely varying degrees. In two countries sales increased by the same small amount: 1 million euros. In Sweden and Denmark, meanwhile, sales rose by less than 1 million euros. By far the greatest increase was in the UK, however, in which sales of Fairtrade coffee rose from 1.5 to 20 million euros. Belgium showed a similar, but less extreme, pattern, with sales rising to nearly 1.7 million euros. Meanwhile, Switzerland maintained its position as the country with the highest sales of Fairtrade coffee, reaching 6 million euros in 2004.

In the second table, which deals with bananas, Switzerland was also the market leader, with sales rising from 15 to 47 million euros. Sales figures for bananas were higher than those for coffee in all five countries, with the exception of Denmark, where coffee sales were slightly higher. Sweden and Denmark showed a different pattern, with banana sales falling to 1 and 0.9 million euros respectively.

Comparing the two tables, it is clear that in 1999 Fairtrade coffee sales ranged from 0.8 to 3 million euros in these five countries, whereas banana sales were much higher, ranging from 0.6 to 15 million euros. By 2004, however, although banana sales had risen in three of the countries to between 4 and 47 million euros, coffee sales had also increased, and this was especially true for Sweden and Denmark which recorded drops in banana sales.

## Examiner's Comment
This model has been prepared by an examiner as an example of a very good answer. However, please note that this is just one example out of many possible approaches.

## Strengths
- Clear overview comparing both products and time periods
- Accurate data reporting with appropriate comparisons
- Effective use of cohesive devices (meanwhile, however, with the exception of)
- Wide range of vocabulary (albeit, market leader, extreme)
- Varied sentence structures throughout
- Well-organised by product type

## Weaknesses
- Could provide more specific data points for some countries
- The conclusion could be slightly more concise
