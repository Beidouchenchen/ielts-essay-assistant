# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 3

## Prompt
The charts below show what UK graduates and postgraduate students who did not go into full-time work after leaving college did in 2008.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Destinations of UK graduates (excluding full-time work) 2008
| Destination | Number |
|-------------|--------|
| Full-time study | 29,815 |
| Further study | 14,745 |
| Voluntary work | 3,555 |
| Combined | 10,535 |

### Destinations of UK postgraduates (excluding full-time work) 2008
| Destination | Number |
|-------------|--------|
| Full-time study | 2,725 |
| Further study | 1,845 |
| Voluntary work | 345 |
| Combined | 1,270 |

## Key Visual Features
- Two bar charts comparing graduate and postgraduate destinations
- Full-time study is the most popular option for both groups
- Graduate numbers are significantly higher than postgraduate numbers
- Voluntary work is the least popular option for both groups
- Combined further study and work is the second most popular option
