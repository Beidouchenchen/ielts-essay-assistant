In the second graph, postgraduates chose to study further full time employment was the most popular with 2,725 postgraduates, and this was more than the figures for choosing further study at just over 1,200. Voluntary work was the least popular for postgraduates as well, with only 345 who were interested in this work. The other students who are a small number of students from both groups did work combined with study, with further study, and with voluntary work, as their destinations after college.

To sum up, it shows that, overall, most of the students who were from both groups preferred to be in the graduate and postgraduate who went to work after leaving college.
