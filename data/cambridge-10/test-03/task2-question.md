# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 10, Test 3

## Prompt
Countries are becoming more and more similar because people are able to buy the same products anywhere in the world.

Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** opinion (positive/negative)
- **Topic:** globalisation and cultural similarity
- **Required stance:** state whether this is positive or negative
- **Requirements:** provide reasons and examples; consider impact of global product availability
