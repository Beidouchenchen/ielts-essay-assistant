# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 9, Test 2

## Prompt
The chart below shows the total number of minutes (in billions) of telephone calls in the UK, divided into three categories, from 1995-2002.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Year | Local-fixed line | National/intl-fixed line | Mobiles |
|------|-----------------|-------------------------|---------|
| 1995 | ~72 | ~38 | ~2 |
| 1996 | ~80 | ~40 | ~5 |
| 1997 | ~85 | ~43 | ~8 |
| 1998 | ~90 | ~45 | ~10 |
| 1999 | ~90 | ~50 | ~12 |
| 2000 | ~85 | ~55 | ~25 |
| 2001 | ~80 | ~60 | ~40 |
| 2002 | ~72 | ~61 | ~46 |

## Key Visual Features
- Grouped bar chart with 3 categories across 8 years
- Local fixed line: peaked ~90bn in 1998-1999, declined to ~72bn by 2002
- National/international: steady growth from ~38bn to ~61bn
- Mobile: dramatic increase from ~2bn to ~46bn, especially 1999-2002
- Gap between categories narrowed significantly over period
