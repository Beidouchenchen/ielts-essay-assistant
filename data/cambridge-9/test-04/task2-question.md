# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 9, Test 4

## Prompt
Every year several languages die out. Some people think that this is not important because life will be easier if there are fewer languages in the world.

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** opinion (agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** language extinction
- **Required stance:** state your degree of agreement/disagreement
- **Requirements:** provide reasons and examples; consider whether fewer languages makes life easier
