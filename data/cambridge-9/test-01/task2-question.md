# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 9, Test 1

## Prompt
Some experts believe that it is better for children to begin learning a foreign language at primary school rather than secondary school.

Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** advantages outweigh disadvantages
- **Topic:** foreign language learning age
- **Required stance:** state whether advantages outweigh disadvantages
- **Requirements:** discuss both advantages and disadvantages of early language learning; provide reasons and examples
