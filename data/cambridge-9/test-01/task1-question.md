# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 9, Test 1

## Prompt
The two maps below show an island, before and after the construction of some tourist facilities.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features
**Before:**
- Island approximately 250 metres long
- Beach on west side
- Palm trees scattered across island
- Surrounded by sea

**After:**
- Beach remains, now labelled 'swimming'
- Accommodation units (huts) built in two areas: west near beach and centre
- Restaurant added in north
- Central reception block added
- Vehicle track connecting reception to pier and accommodation
- Pier added in south with boats
- Footpath connecting western accommodation units to beach
- Palm trees remain
