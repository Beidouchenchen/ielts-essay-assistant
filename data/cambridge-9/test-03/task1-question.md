# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 9, Test 3

## Prompt
The charts below give information on the ages of the populations of Yemen and Italy in 2000 and projections for 2050.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Country | Year | 0-14 years | 15-59 years | 60+ years |
|---------|------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Yemen | 2000 | 50.1% | 46.3% | 3.6% |
| Yemen | 2050 | 37.0% | 57.3% | 5.7% |
| Italy | 2000 | 14.3% | 61.6% | 24.1% |
| Italy | 2050 | 11.5% | 46.2% | 42.3% |

## Key Visual Features
- Four pie charts showing age distribution for two countries across two time points
- Yemen: predominantly young population (50.1% aged 0-14 in 2000), shifting toward older groups by 2050
- Italy: predominantly working-age population (61.6% aged 15-59 in 2000), with significant aging by 2050
- Italy's 60+ group nearly doubles from 24.1% to 42.3%
- Yemen's 0-14 group drops from 50.1% to 37.0%
