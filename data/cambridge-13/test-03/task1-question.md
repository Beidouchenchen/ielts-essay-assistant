# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 3

## Prompt
The bar chart below shows the top ten countries for the production and consumption of electricity in 2014.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Rank | Country | Production (billion kWh) | Consumption (billion kWh) |
|------|---------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | China | 5,398 | 5,322 |
| 2 | United States | 4,099 | 3,866 |
| 3 | Russia | 1,057 | 1,038 |
| 4 | Japan | 936.2 | 856.7 |
| 5 | India | 871 | 698.8 |
| 6 | Canada | 618.9 | 499.9 |
| 7 | France | 561.2 | 462.9 |
| 8 | Brazil | 530.7 | 455.8 |
| 9 | Germany | 526.6 | 582.5 |
| 10 | Korea, Rep. | 485.1 | 449.5 |

## Key Visual Features
- Horizontal bar chart with paired bars (production vs consumption) for 10 countries
- China dominates both categories by a large margin
- Top 2 (China, US) account for vast majority of production
- All countries produce more than they consume except Germany
- Gap between production and consumption varies by country
