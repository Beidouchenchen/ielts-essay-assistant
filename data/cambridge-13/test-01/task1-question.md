# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 1

## Prompt
The two maps below show road access to a city hospital in 2007 and in 2010.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features
- **2007 layout:** City Hospital surrounded by Ring Road; Hospital Rd connects south to City Road; staff and public car park on east side of Hospital Rd; bus stops along Hospital Rd; compass shows N/S/E/W orientation
- **2010 layout:** Same Ring Road and City Hospital; public car park now separate on east side; staff car park repositioned; bus station added on west side; two roundabouts added on Hospital Rd; direct access from Ring Road to public car park added
- **Unchanged features:** City Hospital location, Ring Road, City Road
- **New features (2010):** Public car park (east), bus station (west), two roundabouts on Hospital Rd
