# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 1

## Prompt
Living in a country where you have to speak a foreign language can cause serious social problems, as well as practical problems.

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** opinion (agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** foreign language living abroad
- **Required stance:** state your degree of agreement/disagreement
- **Requirements:** discuss both social and practical problems; provide reasons and examples
