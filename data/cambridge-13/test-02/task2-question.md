# Writing Task 2 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 2

## Prompt
Some people believe that nowadays we have too many choices.

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis
- **Type:** opinion (agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** choices in modern life
- **Required stance:** state your degree of agreement/disagreement
- **Requirements:** provide reasons and examples from knowledge or experience
