# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 2

## Prompt
The chart below shows the percentage of households in owned and rented accommodation in England and Wales between 1918 and 2011.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Year | Owned (%) | Rented (%) |
|------|-----------|------------|
| 1918 | ~22 | ~78 |
| 1939 | ~32 | ~68 |
| 1953 | ~32 | ~68 |
| 1961 | ~42 | ~58 |
| 1971 | ~50 | ~50 |
| 1981 | ~55 | ~45 |
| 1991 | ~69 | ~31 |
| 2001 | ~69 | ~31 |
| 2011 | ~64 | ~36 |

## Key Visual Features
- Bar chart with grouped bars (owned vs rented) for 9 time points
- Clear crossover trend: rented dominant until ~1971, then owned dominant
- Rented peaked at ~78% in 1918, declined to ~31% by 1991/2001
- Owned rose from ~22% in 1918 to peak ~69% in 1991/2001
- Slight reversal after 2001: owned decreased, rented increased
