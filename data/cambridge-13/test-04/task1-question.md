# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 4

## Prompt
The plans below show the layout of a university's sports centre now, and how it will look after redevelopment.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Key Visual Features
**Present layout:**
- Central 25m Pool with Gym above, Seating to right
- Changing room on west side
- Reception at south entrance
- Outdoor courts on both west and east sides
- Compass: N at top

**Future plans:**
- Gym expanded (larger footprint)
- 25m Pool remains in centre
- Seating remains
- Leisure pool added on west side (replacing outdoor courts)
- Sports hall added on east side (replacing outdoor courts)
- 2 Dance studios added on east side (above sports hall)
- Sports shop added near reception (west)
- Cafe added near reception (east)
- Additional Changing room on east side
- Building becomes fully indoor (no outdoor courts)
