# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 8, Test 2

## Prompt
The three pie charts below show the changes in annual spending by a particular UK school in 1981, 1991 and 2001.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Total School Spending 1981
| Category | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Teachers' salaries | 40% |
| Other workers' salaries | 28% |
| Resources (e.g. books) | 15% |
| Furniture and equipment | 15% |
| Insurance | 2% |

### Total School Spending 1991
| Category | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Teachers' salaries | 50% |
| Other workers' salaries | 22% |
| Resources (e.g. books) | 20% |
| Furniture and equipment | 5% |
| Insurance | 3% |

### Total School Spending 2001
| Category | Percentage |
|----------|-----------|
| Teachers' salaries | 45% |
| Other workers' salaries | 15% |
| Resources (e.g. books) | 9% |
| Furniture and equipment | 23% |
| Insurance | 8% |

## Key Visual Features
- Three pie charts showing school spending changes over 20 years
- Teachers' salaries consistently the largest expense (40-50%)
- Other workers' salaries decreased steadily (28% → 22% → 15%)
- Furniture and equipment dropped then rose dramatically (15% → 5% → 23%)
- Resources peaked in 1991 then dropped significantly (15% → 20% → 9%)
- Insurance remained small but grew steadily (2% → 3% → 8%)
