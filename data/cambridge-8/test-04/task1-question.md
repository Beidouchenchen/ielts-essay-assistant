# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 8, Test 4

## Prompt
The graph below shows the quantities of goods transported in the UK between 1974 and 2002 by four different modes of transport.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
| Year | Road | Water | Rail | Pipeline |
|------|------|-------|------|----------|
| 1974 | ~70 | ~40 | ~40 | ~5 |
| 1978 | ~75 | ~40 | ~38 | ~8 |
| 1982 | ~80 | ~45 | ~35 | ~12 |
| 1986 | ~85 | ~50 | ~32 | ~15 |
| 1990 | ~90 | ~55 | ~30 | ~18 |
| 1994 | ~92 | ~58 | ~28 | ~20 |
| 1998 | ~95 | ~60 | ~35 | ~22 |
| 2002 | ~98 | ~62 | ~40 | ~22 |

## Key Visual Features
- Multiple line graph with 4 transport modes across 8 time points
- Road: dominant throughout, steady increase from ~70 to ~98 million tonnes
- Water: second highest, increased from ~40 to ~62 million tonnes
- Rail: relatively stable, slight dip then recovery to ~40 million tonnes
- Pipeline: lowest but significant growth from ~5 to ~22 million tonnes
- Road remained the most popular method throughout the period
