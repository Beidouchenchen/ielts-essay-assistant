# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 8, Test 1

## Prompt
The pie chart below shows the main reasons why agricultural land becomes less productive. The table shows how these causes affected three regions of the world during the 1990s.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Causes of worldwide land degradation
| Cause | Percentage |
|-------|-----------|
| Over-grazing | 35% |
| Deforestation | 30% |
| Over-cultivation | 28% |
| Other | 7% |

### Causes of land degradation by region
| Region | Deforestation | Over-cultivation | Over-grazing | Total land degraded |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| North America | 0.2% | 3.3% | 1.5% | 5% |
| Europe | 9.8% | 7.7% | 5.5% | 23% |
| Oceania | 1.7% | 0% | 11.3% | 13% |

## Key Visual Features
- Pie chart showing over-grazing (35%), deforestation (30%), over-cultivation (28%) as main causes
- Table showing regional differences in degradation causes
- Europe has highest total degradation (23%) with deforestation as main cause
- Oceania mainly affected by over-grazing (11.3%)
- North America has lowest total degradation (5%)
