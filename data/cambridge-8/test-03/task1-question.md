# Writing Task 1 Question
**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 8, Test 3

## Prompt
The diagrams below show the stages and equipment used in the cement-making process, and how cement is used to produce concrete for building purposes.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data
### Cement Production Process
1. Limestone and clay enter crusher
2. Crusher produces powder
3. Powder goes into mixer
4. Mixture passes to rotating heater (uses heat)
5. Material goes to grinder
6. Cement comes out and is packed in bags

### Concrete Production Process
1. Combine: cement (15%), sand (25%), gravel* (50%), water (10%)
2. All materials go into concrete mixer
3. Concrete is produced

*Gravel = small stones

## Key Visual Features
- Two process diagrams showing cement and concrete production
- Cement production: multi-step process using heat (limestone + clay → crusher → mixer → heater → grinder → bags)
- Concrete production: single-step mixing process (cement + sand + gravel + water → mixer)
- Cement production has more steps but fewer materials
- Concrete production has fewer steps but more materials
- Cement production uses heat; concrete production does not
