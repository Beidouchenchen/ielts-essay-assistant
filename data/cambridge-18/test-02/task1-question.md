# Task 1 Question

**Type:** Bar Chart

## Visual Features

- Grouped bar chart showing number of US households by annual income
- Three years: 2007, 2011, 2015
- Five income categories:
  - Less than $25,000
  - $25,000-$49,999
  - $50,000-$74,999
  - $75,000-$99,999
  - $100,000 or more
- Y-axis: Number of households (millions)
- Scale: 0 to 35 million

## Key Data Points

**Less than $25,000:**
- 2007: ~25 million
- 2011: ~27 million
- 2015: ~25 million

**$25,000-$49,999:**

- 2015: ~27 million

**$50,000-$74,999:**

- 2015: ~20 million

**$75,000-$99,999:**
