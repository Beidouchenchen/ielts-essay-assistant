# Writing Task 2 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 18, Test 2

## Prompt

Old people have different needs and interests from younger people, so there is little point in trying to teach the over 60s how to use smartphones and tablets.

To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Question Analysis

- **Type:** Opinion essay (to what extent agree/disagree)
- **Topic:** Teaching technology to elderly people
- **Statement:** Little point teaching over 60s to use smartphones/tablets
- **Required stance:** Agree or disagree (partially or fully)
- **Requirements:** Reasons + examples from knowledge/experience
