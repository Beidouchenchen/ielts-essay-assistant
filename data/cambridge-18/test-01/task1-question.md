# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 18, Test 1

## Prompt

The diagram below shows the process of collecting and bottling natural mineral water.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Process: Natural Mineral Water Bottling

**The Source:**
- Underground springs → pipes → collection tank

**The Factory:**
- Collection tank → sand and stone filters → purification facility
- Purification facility → UV light treatment → storage tanks
- Storage tanks → bottling line → labeled bottles
- Labeled bottles → crates → distribution to retailers

## Key Visual Features

- **Process diagram** with two main sections (source and factory)
- Water collected from underground springs through pipes
- Filtered through sand and stone
- UV light purification treatment
- Bottled, labeled, packed into crates, distributed
