# Writing Task 1 Question

**Source:** Cambridge IELTS 18, Test 4

## Prompt

The bar chart below shows average household income in five cities in the US in 2000, 2005 and 2010.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Data

### Average Household Income (US$) by City

| City | 2000 | 2005 | 2010 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Houston | ~48,000 | ~52,000 | ~48,000 |
| Philadelphia | ~42,000 | ~48,000 | ~40,000 |
| New York | ~55,000 | ~50,000 | ~55,000 |
| San Diego | ~58,000 | ~65,000 | ~60,000 |
| Dallas | ~45,000 | ~55,000 | ~52,000 |

## Key Visual Features

- **Bar chart** with 5 cities × 3 years
- San Diego highest throughout (~58k-65k)
- Philadelphia lowest overall (~40k-48k)
- Dallas and New York in middle range
- Most cities peaked in 2005, then declined slightly
